## Overview

A collection of data-science projects, assignments, and applied challenges — much of it from my
**MSc in Big Data Analytics for Business (IÉSEG School of Management)**, alongside related hands-on
data-science challenges. It spans statistics and econometrics, supervised and unsupervised
learning, forecasting, network analysis, and data engineering, in **Python, R, and SQL**.

## Highlights by topic

**Statistics & econometrics**
- `Boston Regressions.R`, `KNN and GLM Models.R` — regression and generalized linear models.
- `Chow-Lin Interpolation.ipynb` — temporal disaggregation of time series.
- `Statistical Tests using Python.ipynb` — hypothesis testing.

**Supervised learning**
- `Descriptive Analytics and Clasification Model.ipynb`, `Multiple Models.ipynb` — model comparison and evaluation.
- `Hw - Neural Networks.ipynb`, `Transfer_Learning_NN` — neural networks and transfer learning.
- `Rappi - Challenge.ipynb`, `Kaggle_JPR_final.ipynb` — applied classification with hyperparameter tuning and ROC AUC / cross-validation.

**Forecasting & time series**
- `Forecasting - Exam Assignment v2.ipynb`, `Time Series 0722.ipynb`, `Web Scrapping and Time Series.ipynb`.

**Network & social-media analysis**
- `Group Assignment - SNA v2.ipynb` — social network analysis.
- `Social Media Analysis.R`, `Scraping Twitter.py` — social data collection and analysis.

**Data engineering & experimentation**
- `Dataframe manipulation PySpark.py`, `Example of a Pipeline (Data Processing and Cross-Validation Modelling).ipynb`.
- `AB Test.ipynb` — experimental design and A/B testing.

**Applied case studies**
- `Cornershop - Script.ipynb`, `Kueski_0225.ipynb`, `Vinli_0223.ipynb`, `CovidMX.ipynb`.

**Apps & utilities**
- `Shiny App.R` — R Shiny dashboard · `pdf2speech.py` — PDF-to-speech utility.

## Tech Stack

Python · R · SQL · scikit-learn · PySpark · pandas.

> A portfolio-style repository: notebooks are self-contained and meant to demonstrate methods and
> results rather than ship as a single application.
